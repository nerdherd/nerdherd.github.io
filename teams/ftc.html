FTC Team 49, Seven Squared, is a veteran FIRST Tech Challenge team with a strong track record of multiple awards and competitive performance. We are dedicated to developing each members' skills across a wide range of areas, including public speaking, collaboration, and technical expertise in CAD, programming, and assembly, as well as business skills such as maintaining sponsor and community relationships, designing flyers, and managing social media. In the 2024-25 season, we earned First Place Inspire, League Champion, and Winning Alliance Captain honors at ILT, and went on to compete at the Canadian Rockies competition, where we won 2nd Place Think Award and finished as Finalist Captain. In our 2025-26 season has been equally successful, with a Winning Alliance Captain title, First Place Think Award, and League Champion recognition, followed by a 2nd Place Think Award at Canada Cup. At the heart of everything we do is a commitment to team building, guided by our motto: We build people, not just robots.

Hello, we’re FTC 51 NerdHerd Singularity. Our team consists of 2 Captains, 5 sophomore/junior leaders, and 15 new/returning members located in the California Academy of Mathematics and Science in Carson, California. United by our passion for STEM and robotics, we discovered our love for innovation through teamwork and creativity. Together, we are committed to sharing our enthusiasm for FIRST, inspiring others, and celebrating every milestone throughout our journey. With the support of our mentors and sponsors, we strive to embody FIRST’s core values by being inclusive of teammates and respectful competitors at every match. To keep our work neat, our team is organized into three sub-teams: CAD, Programming, and Admin. Leadership is shared between leads, who put emphasis on helping us grow.
